# Recommendations
## E-commerce Funnel Drop-off & RFM Customer Segmentation Analysis

**Analyst:** Prince
**Audience:** Marketing, CRM, and Leadership Teams

---

## How to Read This Document

Each recommendation includes:
- **Action** — what to do
- **Target Segment/Stage** — who it applies to
- **Owner** — who executes it
- **Timeline** — when to launch
- **Success Metric** — how we measure it
- **Expected Impact** — revenue or conversion estimate

---

## Priority 1 — Protect Champions (54% of Revenue)

### Action 1.1: VIP Perks Program (No Discounts)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Target** | Champions segment (961 customers, £9.28M revenue) |
| **Action** | Launch VIP tier: free shipping, early access to new products, exclusive previews, birthday gift |
| **Why** | Champions drive 54% of revenue. Discounts erode margin and train them to wait for sales. Perks cost less and build loyalty. |
| **Owner** | CRM / Loyalty Team |
| **Timeline** | Launch within 30 days |
| **Success Metric** | Champions retention rate (still Champions after 6 months) > 80% |
| **Expected Impact** | Protect £9.28M revenue pool; reduce churn by 5–10% |

### Action 1.2: Personal Outreach to Top 10 Champions

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Target** | Top 10 Champions by revenue |
| **Action** | Account manager personally calls/emails each top customer. Ask for feedback. Offer exclusive perks. |
| **Why** | Top 10 Champions are irreplaceable. Personal touch = highest ROI retention tactic. |
| **Owner** | Head of Customer Success |
| **Timeline** | Within 7 days |
| **Success Metric** | 100% contacted; 80%+ response rate |
| **Expected Impact** | Protect highest-value relationships |

| **Owner** | CRM Team |

## Priority 3 — Fix the Funnel (Conversion)

### Action 3.1: Second-Purchase Incentive (Stage 1 → 2)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Target** | First-time buyers (1,625 customers dropped off here) |
| **Action** | Automated email within 7 days of first purchase: "Here's 10% off your next order — expires in 14 days" |
| **Why** | Biggest absolute loss (1,625 customers). Second purchase = 3x more likely to become repeat buyer. |
| **Owner** | Marketing Automation |
| **Timeline** | Launch within 14 days |
| **Success Metric** | Repeat buyer conversion from 72.27% → 78%+ |
| **Expected Impact** | ~300 additional Repeat Buyers |

### Action 3.2: Loyalty Tier Program (Stage 2 → 3)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Target** | Repeat Buyers (4,236 customers) |
| **Action** | Points-based loyalty program: earn at 3 orders, unlock free shipping at 5 orders |
| **Why** | 49.46% drop-off here. Tier rewards give repeat buyers a reason to keep coming back. |
| **Owner** | Loyalty / CRM Team |
| **Timeline** | Launch within 60 days |
| **Success Metric** | Loyal Buyer conversion from 50.54% → 58%+ |
| **Expected Impact** | ~300 additional Loyal Buyers |

### Action 3.3: VIP Milestone at 10 Orders (Stage 3 → 4)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Target** | Loyal Buyers (2,141 customers) |
| **Action** | At 10th order: unlock VIP status (free shipping, early access, exclusive products) |
| **Why** | Biggest % drop-off (55.11%). Loyal buyers need a clear milestone to become Champions. |
| **Owner** | CRM / Loyalty Team |
| **Timeline** | Launch within 60 days |
| **Success Metric** | Champion conversion from 44.89% → 52%+ |
| **Expected Impact** | ~150 additional Champions |
